## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose a digital platform called FarmConnect that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers.
- Farmers can register and upload their available products, quantity, and prices.
- Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products directly from farmers.
- Bulk buyers can order products in large quantities.
- The platform removes unnecessary middlemen and reduces extra costs.
- Customers can track their orders and delivery status.
- AI helps farmers predict demand and decide suitable prices.
- A delivery system connects farmers with nearby transport services.

## Technical Approach
The proposed system uses modern web technologies and Artificial Intelligence.

Technologies Used:
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap.
- Backend: Python and Django.
- Database: SQLite / MySQL.
- AI/ML: Python for demand forecasting.
- Maps: Google Maps API for route optimization.
- Payment: UPI and online payment integration.
Implementation Methodology:
1. Farmer and customer registration.
2. Farmers upload products and prices.
3. Customers search and order products.
4. The system processes orders and payments.
5. AI predicts product demand.
6. Route optimization identifies efficient delivery routes.
7. Customers track their orders.
## Feasibility and Viability
The proposed FarmConnect platform is practical and affordable because it uses commonly available technologies.

Feasibility:
- Can be developed using Python and Django.
- Farmers can access the platform using smartphones.
- Online payments make transactions easier.
- Cloud hosting supports future expansion.
Potential Challenges:
- Lack of digital knowledge among some farmers.
- Internet connectivity problems in rural areas.
- Transportation and delivery difficulties.
- Product quality and price differences.
Solutions to Challenges:
- Provide a simple interface in regional languages.
- Support mobile-friendly and low-internet usage.
- Partner with local delivery services.
- Provide product ratings and quality verification.
## Impact and Benefits
Economic Benefits:
- Farmers receive better prices for their products.
- Consumers get agricultural products at lower prices.
- Reduces unnecessary middlemen and commissions.
- Creates new business opportunities for farmers.
Social Benefits:
- Supports rural farmers and FPOs.
- Improves farmers' digital knowledge.
- Connects rural and urban communities.
- Provides equal market opportunities.
Environmental Benefits:
- Reduces food wastage.
- Optimizes delivery routes and fuel usage.
- Encourages local agricultural distribution.
- Reduces unnecessary transportation.
Overall Impact: FarmConnect creates a transparent agricultural marketplace that benefits farmers, consumers, and the environment.
